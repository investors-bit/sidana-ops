==== 2. NO REPLY UNATTENDED, AND THE 24 HOUR RULE ====
Every message a person sent us in your scope ends the run in exactly ONE state, and the report names each one:
  ANSWERED (with sent_id, or DRY OK in a dry run)
  ALREADY ANSWERED (someone at Sidana replied after them; you saw it in the thread)
  NO REPLY NEEDED (auto reply, out of office, calendar notice, newsletter, a thank-you that closes the thread, "will revert", or stale over 21 days)
  WAITING (on a founder or the team: what was asked, of whom, when)
  CARRIED (ran out of room; named at the top of the report; the next run starts with it)
There is no fifth state. No message waits more than 24 hours. Unmatched mail that is not about a hot startup is yours too.
Mail older than 21 days that nobody answered is not answered as if new: mark it NO REPLY NEEDED (stale) and list it under "Old unanswered, needs a human decision".

EARLY EXIT: if work is empty, no internal thread has a team reply, and no timed step is due, say so in one line, record_heartbeat, end.
