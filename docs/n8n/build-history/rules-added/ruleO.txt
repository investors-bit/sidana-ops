
O. QUESTIONS WE CANNOT ANSWER: FOUNDER FIRST, THEN THE TEAM (Aditya, 13 October). When an investor asks a question about a startup and the answer is not in STARTUPS, STARTUP_PROFILE, the documents sheet or the thread:
1. Ask the founder the same run, from the mailbox that owns the startup, quoting the investor's question word for word and saying an investor is asking. Then tell the investor on their thread that you are checking and will come back to them. Record it as an open ask.
2. When the founder answers, reply to the investor the same run with the founder's answer, using only what the founder wrote, and close the open ask.
3. If the founder has not answered 2 days (48 hours) after you asked, send ONE internal_mail to investors@sidanaventures.com, dealflow@sidanaventures.com and shradhaa@sidanaventures.com, subject exactly ANSWER NEEDED | <Startup> <> <Investor>. Body: the investor and the person, the question word for word, the founder's name and phone number from Founders Info, when you asked the founder, and one line asking them to reply on this same email with the answer. This is a fourth allowed kind of email to Aditya, next to TIME NEEDED, TRAIL APPROVAL and BOOK VIA LINK. Mark the item WAITING on the team.
4. Every run, read your own ANSWER NEEDED threads with mail_history (subject_like "ANSWER NEEDED"). When anyone on the team replies with the answer, use it to answer the investor on their thread the same run, without saying who inside Sidana supplied it, and close the item. If the founder answers in the meantime, use the founder's answer and tell the team on the ANSWER NEEDED thread that it is no longer needed.
5. If nobody on the team has replied one working day after the ANSWER NEEDED mail, send one short nudge on that same thread, then raise it at the top of your report every run until it is answered. Never guess an answer and never send a number or fact that did not come from the founder, the team or the book.
