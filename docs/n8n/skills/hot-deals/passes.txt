PASSES (hot deals).
Lines like "we have decided not to proceed", "not in our thesis", "will pass", "not a fit", "beyond our current focus" are passes. An explicit pass waiting on us is closed THIS RUN.

FIRST decide the kind: was a meeting on this deal with this investor held? Look at meetings_held on the row, a HELD, TRAILED or WATCHING stage, or a past meeting in work_queue meetings_past_48h / the meetings query.

KIND A, PASS BEFORE ANY MEETING. Reply on their thread: short, warm, no argument, no re-pitch, say we will keep sending curated deals. Then write_pipeline on that row: investor_response, pass_reason (their words, verbatim), deal_status "Closed - passed", next_action "Closed - passed", next_due "", pause "Yes". The deal flow agent runs the thesis loop for every firm and counts kind A passes on hot deals too, so record every kind A pass accurately. You do not ask for thesis calls yourself, but you DO ask the rule Y question and write any thesis they give (below).
KIND B, PASS AFTER A MEETING. Thank them properly for their time and their feedback on the existing thread. Then write_pipeline: pass_reason verbatim, meeting_outcome "Passed after meeting: <their reason in their own words>", last_contact today's date (YYYY-MM-DD), deal_status and next_action "Closed - passed", next_due "", pause "Yes", current_stage "CLOSED_PASS". Do not email the founder; the Saturday weekly founder report carries it. Then stop completely on that pair.
A pass closes every row for that firm on that startup. Send first, then write the row. In a dry run, do not write.

HYGIENE AUDIT ROWS (hygiene_audit {scope:"hot"} returns passed rows still being chased): write_pipeline with allow_sent:true, set next_action "Closed - passed", next_due "", deal_status "Closed - passed", pause "Yes", append_notes:true noting what next_action was before. Call hygiene_audit again and confirm zero. Report the number fixed.

FEEDBACK AFTER EVERY PASS (core rule Y) AND FUNDS (core rule Z). Kind A with no clear reason: in the same thank-you reply ask the one question, thesis fit or something about this company. Kind B (after a meeting), or any thread where a founder is copied: NEVER ask the question; just thank them. Once per investor per startup. Their answer decides: a thesis answer goes into the sheet (below); a company reason goes in pass_reason verbatim, and in meeting_outcome as "Feedback: <reason>" if no meeting was held. Each time you ask: one FEEDBACK_CHASE diary entry 7 days out, in the same item (step 5b closes it instead if they answered). Write the question fresh each time; one question per person per run. Out of funds: core rule Z (NEW_FUND_CHECK on the 1st of the month they name, else 90 days).

WRITING A THESIS INTO THE SHEET (core rule AA). Find their INVESTOR_CRITERIA row with read_book by investor name (column B must equal the INVESTORS name exactly). Read the row, then write_cells with expect = what you just read. Max 20 cells.
Columns: B investor, D status (keep ACTIVE), E confirmed (keep AUTO; never write PENDING), G sector_mode (AGNOSTIC or LIST), H sectors_in, I sectors_excluded, J models_in, K models_excluded, L stage_min, M stage_max, N cheque_min_cr (number, INR Cr), O arr_min_cr (number), T geography_scope, V thesis_summary, Z rule_notes, AF round_max_cr (number, the largest round they will join).
Lists are comma separated codes. Never invent a code. Excluded lists: ADD to what is there, never remove.
