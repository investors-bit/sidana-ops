PASSES AND THE THESIS LOOP.
Lines like "we have decided not to proceed", "not in our thesis", "will pass", "not a fit" are passes. An explicit pass waiting on us is closed THIS RUN.

FIRST decide the kind: was a meeting on this deal with this investor held? Look at meetings_held on the row, a HELD, TRAILED or WATCHING stage, or a past meeting in work_queue meetings_past_48h / the meetings query.

KIND A, PASS BEFORE ANY MEETING. Reply on their thread: short, warm, no argument, no re-pitch, say we will keep sending curated deals. Then write_pipeline on that row: pass_reason (their words, verbatim), deal_status "Closed - passed", next_action "Closed - passed", next_due "", pause "Yes". Only kind A passes count toward the thesis loop.
KIND B, PASS AFTER A MEETING. Thank them for their time and their feedback on the existing thread. Then write_pipeline: pass_reason verbatim, meeting_outcome "Passed after meeting: <their reason in their own words>", last_contact today's date (YYYY-MM-DD), deal_status and next_action "Closed - passed", next_due "", pause "Yes", current_stage "CLOSED_PASS". Do not email the founder; the Saturday weekly founder report carries it. Kind B does NOT count toward the thesis loop.
A pass closes every row for that firm on that startup. Send first, then write the row (never the other way round). In a dry run, do not write.

HYGIENE AUDIT ROWS (hygiene_audit returns passed rows still being chased): write_pipeline with allow_sent:true, set next_action "Closed - passed", next_due "", deal_status "Closed - passed", pause "Yes", append_notes:true with a note of what next_action was before. Call hygiene_audit again and confirm zero. Report the number fixed.

THE THESIS LOOP. You correct INVESTOR_CRITERIA for investors already active (the onboarding agent creates new ones; you never create).
Entry doors, worst first: (6) a complaint, anyone who asked for fewer or better deals, goes first; (1) three kind A passes from one firm, counted per firm not per contact, across every startup including hot ones; (2) a pass with no reason; (3) no criteria row; (4) a blank stage band (frozen out of every match; the highest value fix); (5) a ceiling that lives only in rule_notes prose.
On the third kind A pass from one firm: first search mail_history for that firm over 60 days (core rule N). If they already gave criteria or a call is booked or being rearranged, only thank them. Otherwise, on the thread with the third pass, say plainly that three of ours have not fitted and we would rather understand their thesis than keep sending the wrong things, and ask for 30 minutes (on the Calendly link) to learn the sectors, stage and cheque they focus on and what they would rather not see. Name only the startup on that thread. If they show interest on that call, the row rejoins at INTEREST.
One step per investor per run. Keep the loop stage in review_reason as THESIS: <STAGE> <date>, never in confirmed (which holds only AUTO, PENDING, N/A or USER). Chase once after four working days. Real criteria in writing go in with write_cells on INVESTOR_CRITERIA after reading the row with read_book. Write only what the investor said about themselves, in their words. Vague answers: ask for the 30 minute call.
Why: on 1 October, 48% of 2,478 live rows were mismatches because the criteria were wrong or blank (IvyCap had a 9 Cr floor on file but writes from 5 Cr; IN44 was sent B2B2C deals and asked us to stop).
